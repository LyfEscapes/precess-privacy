What Precess is

Precess is a 3D action game. You run on stone rings that keep turning inside each other while the stone heats up and melts. Gather every shard to wake the gate, step through, and drop to the next ring. The game plays offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

How to play

  • Run with the pad at the lower left, or swipe on the game and hold. Step over an edge and gravity follows you onto the next side.
  • Tap the screen or Leap to jump one tile. Where two rings cross, stand on the glowing tile and Leap to cross onto the other ring.
  • Twist turns the section you stand on a half-turn.
  • The round buttons at the lower right turn the view left and right, and change the view: close, first person, or wide to see every ring and shard.
  • Flickering tiles are about to melt, and molten tiles burn. Don't stand still: the stone heats wherever you wait, and the ring erupts when the heat bar is full.
  • Collect every shard shown at the top right. The gate then opens. Reach it to escape to the next ring.
  • With a hardware keyboard: W A S D or arrows to run, Shift to leap, Space to twist, Q and E to turn the view, V to change it, P to pause.

Common problems

Blank or black screen. Close the app fully and open it again. If it shows "The rings did not load", tap to try again. If it stays blank, restart your device and install any update for Precess and for your system. If it happens again, email us.

No sound. Tap the Sound button at the top right during a run. Turn up the media volume, and check silent mode and any connected headphones. If you chose to play with sound off, tap the Sound button to turn it on.

Lost progress. Precess remembers only the deepest ring you reached, on your device. We have no accounts and can't see or recover it. Deleting the app deletes it.

A video gave no extra life. Keep going needs the video to play to the end. If no video can load at all, the extra life is given anyway.

Restoring Remove ads

  • Android: use the Google account that made the purchase and tap Restore on the title screen.
  • iPhone and iPad: use the Apple Account that made the purchase and tap Restore purchases on the title screen.

Purchases belong to the store where you bought them, so they don't move between Android and Apple devices.

Refunds

The store takes the payment and decides on refunds.

  • Android: request a Google Play refund.
  • iPhone and iPad: request an Apple refund.

Ads and privacy choices

Precess is free with ads. The video for Keep going is optional, and a full-screen ad can appear between runs or as you reach deeper rings. Remove ads stops those ads and makes Keep going free.

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: allow or stop tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Where Google's consent rules require it, an Ad privacy button appears on the title screen.
