Precess

Privacy Policy · LyfEscapes LLC · Effective November 25, 2026

Precess is a game by LyfEscapes LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Precess on Android, iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

We receive nothing. Precess has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of our own and no servers of ours, so we never receive your name, email, contacts, photos, files, location or any identifier for your device. The ads in the game are different: Google's ad service collects some information from your device, as described under Ads below.

What stays on your device

The deepest ring you have reached and whether you bought "Remove ads" are stored only on your device, so the game remembers them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage on Android, or deleting the app on iPhone or iPad, deletes them. A purchase can be restored afterwards with the Restore button on Android or Restore purchases on iPhone and iPad.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Precess shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for one more life when a run is over, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs or between deeper rings. The game itself still works offline; the internet is only used for ads and purchases. To choose and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its service, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device and shares it with Google:

  • device identifiers: on Android the advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location, estimated from your IP address,
  • app interactions such as launches and ad views, and advertising data such as the ads you see and tap, and
  • crash, performance and other diagnostic information about the app and the SDK.

This information goes to Google, not to us. It is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the app shows Google's consent message before ads load. Where Google requires a way to change that choice later, an Ad privacy button appears on the title screen. You can also limit ad data in your device's settings, as described under Your choices.

Tracking on iPhone and iPad

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks your permission before it can use your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. The game plays the same whether you allow it or not. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases

Precess offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads", which removes the full-screen ads and makes the extra life free. On Android, payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. On iPhone and iPad, it is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether you own the purchase.

Links to other Escapes games (Android)

In the Android app, "The Escapes series" on the title screen lists the other games in the series, and each one opens that game's page on Google Play. Nothing is sent to us when you use it. The iPhone and iPad app has no such links.

Google Play and the App Store

You download Precess from Google Play or from Apple's App Store. Google and Apple may collect information about installs, crashes and usage under their own privacy policies; we only see anonymous totals in their developer tools.

Children

Precess is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove what is on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage on Android, or delete the app on iPhone or iPad. To limit ad data, use the Ad privacy button in the game where it appears, and:

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: allow or stop tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
